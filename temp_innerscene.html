Create a Model with AI

Can’t find the part you need? Describe it, or add a photo, and AI builds a 3D model in the 3D viewer at real size. Refine it by asking for changes, then export it to STEP, STL, DWG, FBX or another format. It runs on your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key; see how to get a key and what it costs.

Contribute

Upload models and drawings (or a zip of them) and describe each one. The viewer sorts each file into the 3D or the drawing library. Everything is reviewed before it appears, released under CC0, and kept for 1 to 5 years depending on file size. See the library terms.
