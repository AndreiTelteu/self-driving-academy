## Contract

Statuses: CHANGED, NO_CHANGE, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, REJECTED, ERROR.

Rules: CHANGED requires at least one change and the real 103 composition versionId. NO_CHANGE and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE require versionId null, zero changes and a closed reason. ERROR and REJECTED require zero changes, no version and the real closed reason/detail. every change carries a positive effective episode count, real contexts, quality, uncertainty and confidence. a real zero is a valid value and never an unobserved one. a late or foreign job becomes a closed STALE_JOB error, never the current result. an R key can never appear as a learned change. the panel writes only changed text at 10 Hz and never invents a version, value or publication.

Limits: 24 change rows, 8 contexts per change, 8 missing-context entries and a 10 Hz write ceiling.
