Installed0.21 narrow-phase implementation frees its temporary manifold and contact pair only after the callback returns, without try/finally. Native scenario expectations for later executed tests:

- Wall and vehicle impact produce a mapped pair with a finite positive summed normal impulse after a real fixed physics step; no invented force-to-impulse conversion.
- Ground support, sensors, unnamed boxes and obstacle–obstacle pairs produce no COLLISION incidents. Their diagnostic counters are distinct from accepted mapped contacts.
- A mapped touching pair emits once. Persistence beyond cooldown does not repeat it. Complete separation followed by recontact at the cooldown boundary can emit a second incident; a recontact during cooldown is suppressed for that entire episode.
- Reversed pair enumeration and multiple manifolds aggregate once by canonical registration tokens. Removal/re-registration, native handle reuse and world replacement cannot revive an old pair or pending incident.
- Every contact snapshot, active pair, cooldown record and pending publication queue has a finite admission bound. Overflow rejects the whole proposed update before state mutation; complete snapshots are never silently truncated.

Pure vehicles records must not import simulation/events or EventBus: simulation already imports vehicles. Composition translates exact007 COLLISION fields and stable event IDs. Accepted-prefix publication, duplicate retry and listener failures require explicit tests; accepted bus effects cannot be rolled back or blindly repeated. No arbitrary epoch increment may bypass capacity. Final ownership probes cover repeated world create/dispose and core reset/dispose with all application-owned contact/pair/pending counters returning to zero.

Registration identity must be a canonical owned token, checked by reference against its owning live world. Native Rapier handles are finite numeric encodings, not guaranteed safe integers; they are lookup values, never incident identities. Each new registration has a monotonic safe-integer serial. Entity names are unique across vehicle and named-obstacle kinds; static obstacle mapping cannot depend on a parent rigid body. Removal retires the exact collider token and clears related active/cooldown/pending records. The canonical unordered token pair determines deduplication, while a two-vehicle incident chooses its primary vehicle deterministically by entity ID. Event payload intensity is the summed nonnegative normal impulse for the admitted actual contacts on the onset tick, not cumulative energy, force or per-frame repeated damage.

Core admission requires a session identity of at most256 code units, consecutive tick snapshots after the first, and synchronous publication before admission of a newer tick when incidents remain pending. Equivalent same-tick retries are idempotent. Incident IDs include session namespace, world epoch, canonical registration pair and onset tick. Separated history expires at `lastIncidentTick + cooldownTicks`; present episodes remain tracked even when cooldown has elapsed, including a recontact suppressed during cooldown.

Native admitted interaction is `contactDist <= normalizedAllowedLinearError * lengthUnit` OR a strictly positive actual normal impulse. This includes predictive CCD impulses at positive geometric gaps and native resting tolerance (default1mm); it is not a penetration-only claim. Positive gaps beyond tolerance with zero impulse are excluded by an executed native test. Readback enumerates car candidates only: obstacle–obstacle zero means not enumerated, and sensor zero does not prove sensor absence because sensor intersections use another native query. Executed exclusion scenarios establish no incident instead.
