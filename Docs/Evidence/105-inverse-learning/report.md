## Limitations

- Synthetic, deterministic observation streams, not captured gameplay or browser sessions.
- The harness feeds the real estimators but supplies the 086/087/088 records itself; no worker,
  profile store, publication, ledger write or MANUAL/AUTO learning path is exercised.
- Opportunity kinds are identity carriers from the closed 086 vocabulary; the vocabulary has no
  FOLLOWING kind, so leader/queue episodes attach to GREEN/STOP opportunities.
- Interval keys (yield and lane gaps) are validated against their observed interval; the midpoint is
  never reported as a precise point estimate.
- Tolerances are documented starting values for calibration, not statistical confidence intervals.

## Tolerance model

Effective bound: `absolute + relative × |target|`. Every row is key-specific; there is no uniform default.

| Key | Absolute | Relative | Justification |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| speed_delta_urban | 0.2 | 0.05 | Speed samples are quantized per 20 Hz frame and the median absorbs free-flow spread; 0.2 m/s covers the posting-limit rounding (0.1 m/s) plus the ±0.04 m/s seeded sample noise, and 5% covers higher urban limits. |
| speed_delta_residential | 0.2 | 0.05 | Residential limits are lower, so the same 0.2 m/s posting/quantization floor dominates; the relative part stays at 5% because the estimator uses the same median over a 10 s window. |
| curve_lateral_accel | 0.25 | 0.08 | The value is reconstructed from p90 class-normalized lateral demand times the 023 sedan ceiling (grip 1.3 × 9.81 m/s²), so the demand percentile and the ceiling rounding dominate; 8% covers demand variation across curve radii. |
| intersection_approach_speed | 0.4 | 0.03 | An approach window contains deceleration, so the median is a spread statistic rather than a single cruise value; 0.4 m/s covers the approach profile spread and 3% covers faster arterials. |
| desired_acceleration | 0.15 | 0.05 | Demand is a dimensionless p90 fraction mapped to the sedan reference ceiling (6500 N / 1400 kg ≈ 4.643 m/s²); 0.15 m/s² covers the trimmed p50 and the class-capability rounding, 5% covers the demand spread. |
| comfort_deceleration | 0.2 | 0.05 | The deceleration ceiling is 8 m/s², so one demand percentage point is 0.08 m/s²; 0.2 m/s² covers the trimmed p50 plus the p90-to-p50 difference and 5% covers stronger braking. |
| acceleration_jerk | 0.3 | 0.1 | Jerk is differenced at the fixed 60 Hz tick, so a single-frame acceleration change is amplified; 0.3 m/s³ covers the 1/60 s differencing noise and 10% covers the outer-tail trimming of the p50. |
| brake_reaction_delay | 0.02 | 0.02 | The reaction is an integer tick count divided by 60, so one tick is 0.0167 s; the 0.02 s absolute floor is exactly that quantization and 2% keeps the bound proportional for longer reactions. |
| following_time_headway | 0.08 | 0.03 | The OLS slope divides gap quantization (0.01 m seeded noise) by the ego-speed spread (≥15 m/s), giving ~0.001 s; 0.08 s covers gap measurement and lateral offset in the fitted line, 3% covers headway spread. |
| following_min_gap | 0.15 | 0.03 | The intercept is the fitted gap at 0 m/s, which extrapolates from the lowest observed speed; 0.15 m covers that extrapolation plus the RMS residual rounding and 3% covers larger gaps. |
| queue_standstill_gap | 0.15 | 0.03 | The queue value is the arithmetic mean of measured standstill gaps, so the 0.01 m seeded measurement noise and the 0.1 m/s stop qualification define a 0.15 m floor; 3% covers longer vehicles. |
| following_speed_gain | 0.05 | 0.03 | Speed gain is a mean of observed per-episode ratios in 1/s, not a fit coefficient; 0.05 covers the per-episode observation rounding and 3% covers gains above 1/s. |
| red_stop_probability | 0.03 | 0.02 | The value is a ratio over a certain opportunity count, so its granularity is 1/N; 0.03 is smaller than one count at the fixture denominators (N ≥ 15) and 2% covers stricter fixtures, not a statistical confidence interval. |
| green_start_delay | 0.02 | 0.03 | Onset and departure are integer ticks at 60 Hz, so the mean carries a 0.0167 s quantization; 0.02 s is that floor and 3% covers fixtures with few departures. |
| red_stop_line_offset | 0.1 | 0.03 | The stop-line offset is the mean of metre measurements rounded to 0.05 m; 0.1 m is two rounding steps and 3% covers larger offsets. |
| late_red_brake_threshold | 0.05 | 0.04 | The threshold is a mean of deceleration measurements rounded to 0.05 m/s²; 0.05 is exactly one rounding step and 4% covers thresholds near the 6 m/s² upper bound. |
| stop_full_probability | 0.03 | 0.02 | A full-stop ratio shares the 1/N opportunity granularity; 0.03 is below one count at N ≥ 15 and 2% covers the stricter 1/20 fixtures, without inventing a binomial interval. |
| stop_dwell_time | 0.02 | 0.03 | Dwell is measured in whole ticks at 60 Hz, so the mean has a 0.0167 s quantization; 0.02 s is that floor and 3% covers dwell times above 1 s. |
| stop_line_offset | 0.1 | 0.03 | Same metre measurement as the RED offset but taken at a STOP line; 0.1 m covers two 0.05 m rounding steps and 3% covers offsets of several metres. |
| yield_time_gap | 0.15 | 0.05 | Only opposed YIELDED/PROCEEDED facts identify the threshold, and the result is an interval whose endpoints are measured separations; 0.15 s pads the interval-edge rounding and 5% covers wider separations. |
| lane_change_front_gap | 0.3 | 0.04 | The front gap is measured at the manoeuvre and only an accepted/rejected interval identifies the threshold; 0.3 m covers the gap measurement rounding and 4% covers gaps of tens of metres. |
| lane_change_back_gap | 0.3 | 0.04 | The back gap uses the same manoeuvre-time measurement as the front gap but a different reference vehicle; 0.3 m covers its own rounding and 4% covers the larger gaps. |
| lane_change_speed_gain | 0.15 | 0.05 | The advantage is a speed difference in m/s recorded per optional manoeuvre; 0.15 m/s covers the difference rounding and 5% covers advantages near 10 m/s. |
| lane_change_cooldown | 0.05 | 0.03 | The cooldown is a measured tick interval divided by 60; 0.05 s allows for manoeuvre-boundary tick assignment and 3% covers long cooldowns up to 30 s. |
