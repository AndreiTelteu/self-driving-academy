# Blocked-route recovery and MANUAL→AUTO re-entry 054

Status: implementation with targeted local validation. PBI054 board state and publication are owned by the parent; the cost/memory probe, the tests and the limitations are recorded in [the 054 evidence](Evidence/054-blocked-recovery/README.md).

`evaluateBlockedRecovery` is a pure 10 Hz decision that feeds the existing 045 BLOCKED state instead of introducing a second state machine. It owns no clock, no planner, no physical body, no authoritative pose writer and no route search. Lack of progress is derived from two authoritative inputs only: the subject's longitudinal speed and the real 033 `locateLane` query on the authoritative pose. `blockedRecoveryBehaviorFacts` raises `BehaviorFacts.routeBlocked` and nothing else, so the closed 045 reason set stays intact and the distinguishable cause lives in the 054 diagnostic.

The input schema is sessionId, worldEpoch, actor, decisionTick, graph, observation and state. `graph` is the real 033 lane graph; when it is missing the module abstains (`MISSING_LANE_GRAPH`, `UNKNOWN`) rather than claiming the vehicle left the graph. `observation` is the authoritative progress view at a tick: access, `speedMps`, the own `laneId`, the read-only `positionM`/`headingRad`, the 044 leader identity/gap/speed, the real 041 `routeStatus` of the current route, the lane the caller already knows to be untraversable (`blockedLaneAheadId`), the explicit 041 planner context (origin, destination, access, graph/cost version, blocked set) and the host policy flag `allowRecoveryEscalation`. Unknown or stale data (older than six ticks, or from the future) abstains with `NO_OBSERVATION`/`OBSERVATION_UNAVAILABLE` and no fabricated fact.

Cause priority is deterministic and each cause is a separate enumerated reason: off the 033 corridors (`NO_PROGRESS_OFF_GRAPH`), a blocked lane ahead (`NO_PROGRESS_BLOCKED_LANE_AHEAD`), no feasible successor while the route itself is unusable (`NO_PROGRESS_NO_FEASIBLE_SUCCESSOR`), a route the 041 planner cannot serve (`NO_PROGRESS_ROUTE_BLOCKED`), a queue standstill past the threshold (`NO_PROGRESS_LEADER_STANDSTILL`) and stagnation with no explained cause (`NO_PROGRESS_UNEXPLAINED`). Structural causes block immediately; the dynamic one needs `stagnationTicksToBlock` (120 ticks, 2 s) of authoritative no-progress. A terminal lane is a legitimate arrival, never a "no feasible successor" block, and a red signal or a local gap never becomes a cause by itself.

The recovery policy is an explicit ordered table. `blockedSinceTick` is the first tick of the episode, escalation inside one episode is monotone (`NONE`→`WAITING`→`REROUTE`→`RECOVERY`/`STAY_BLOCKED`) and can never regress, and the release requires `releaseHoldTicks` (12 ticks) of continuous authoritative progress, so a brief speed blip cannot reset the episode budget or oscillate the state every decision slice.

| Cause | First action | Escalation | Terminal limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PROGRESS` | `CONTINUE` | episode released after `releaseHoldTicks` | — |
| `STAGNATION_WITHIN_THRESHOLD` | `WAIT` | becomes a block at the declared threshold | — |
| `NO_PROGRESS_LEADER_STANDSTILL` | `WAIT` | after `waitTicksBeforeReroute`: `REQUEST_REROUTE` | `STAY_BLOCKED` or `REQUEST_RECOVERY` |
| `NO_PROGRESS_UNEXPLAINED` | `WAIT` | as above | `STAY_BLOCKED` or `REQUEST_RECOVERY` |
| `NO_PROGRESS_BLOCKED_LANE_AHEAD` | `REQUEST_REROUTE` (immediate) | cooldown-separated bounded attempts | `STAY_BLOCKED` or `REQUEST_RECOVERY` |
| `NO_PROGRESS_NO_FEASIBLE_SUCCESSOR` | `REQUEST_REROUTE` (immediate) | as above | `STAY_BLOCKED` or `REQUEST_RECOVERY` |
| `NO_PROGRESS_ROUTE_BLOCKED` | `REQUEST_REROUTE` (immediate) | as above | `STAY_BLOCKED` or `REQUEST_RECOVERY` |
| `NO_PROGRESS_OFF_GRAPH` | `REQUEST_REROUTE` with a planner context, otherwise `REQUEST_RECOVERY` | as above | `ESCALATION_NOT_PERMITTED` / `NO_RECOVERY_TARGET` |

`REQUEST_REROUTE` never searches a second time. The descriptor carries origin, destination, access, the caller's graph version, the caller's blocked set extended with the observed blocked lane, and a `costVersion` derived deterministically by `blockedRerouteCostVersion` from the base version, the blocked-set signature and the attempt index; the owner applies it to the real 041 planner (`setBlocked`, `setVersions`) and drives the real 219 route port, so a stale cached route for a different blockage or an earlier attempt can never be reused. Each episode is bounded to `maximumRerouteAttempts` (2) requests separated by `rerouteCooldownTicks` (300 ticks, 5 s). After the budget is spent the module escalates or stays blocked; it never keeps retrying silently and it never invents a route.

`REQUEST_RECOVERY` is the explicit 030 escalation and the last resort. Its `road` field is exactly 030's `RecoveryRoadProof` (`mapId`, `laneId`, `access`, `start`, `end`, `widthM`) built from the real 033 directed path and lane width, so the host can hand it to the existing native port unchanged. The module only requests: the physical inspection, placement and native commit remain 030's, including its requirement of an explicit controlled MANUAL/LEARNING seat. When the host policy `allowRecoveryEscalation` is false, or when no 033 lane is known to recover onto, the outcome is an explicit terminal `STAY_BLOCKED` naming `ESCALATION_NOT_PERMITTED` or `NO_RECOVERY_TARGET`.

`evaluateAuthorityReentry` covers the MANUAL/LEARNING→AUTO handover. While authority is MANUAL or LEARNING the vehicle belongs to the driver and the module reports `MANUAL_AUTHORITY` without planning over him. When AUTO returns, the real 033 `locateLane` decides: well-aligned yields `CONTINUE`/`ON_LANE`; inside the corridor but off centre beyond `reentryLateralToleranceM` (1 m) yields `DRIVE_TO_LANE` with the target lane, distance, real lateral offset and heading error, and only when the real 041 planner proved that route (`routeStatus === 'READY'`); off every corridor yields an explicit 030 `REQUEST_RECOVERY` when the host permits it, and otherwise an explicit bounded failure (`REENTRY_IMPOSSIBLE` with `RECOVERY_DISALLOWED`, `NO_RECOVERY_TARGET` or `ROUTE_NOT_PROVEN`). A null route status is never a silent success. The decision type carries the literal `teleportsVehicle: false` and no position/transform field at all: the driving chain 046–049/024 owns the physical move back to the lane, and 030 owns any physical placement. This module has no write path to the authoritative pose.

Admission bounds: 110 actors, at most 64 blocked lanes per request, six-tick observation age, 256/128 UTF-16 units, speeds 0–100 m/s, displacement bounding of the blocked set by exact signature, zero retained actors/episodes/decisions. The caller holds the `BlockedRecoveryState` latch (nine primitive fields) and carries it forward; rejection is atomic and does not invoke accessors.

Evidence at commit `9038013e`: 840 project tests pass (832 existing plus seven isolated 054 tests and one real 219/044/045/033/041 chain test). The isolated tests cover the abstention paths, the queue standstill that blocks exactly at the declared threshold and then requests a real reroute within the documented wait and cooldown bounds, the blocked lane ahead whose request actually changes the planner path to the legal alternative, every structural cause including the arrival-not-a-block case, the hysteresis that keeps one episode across a progress blip and releases only after the declared hold, and the impossible re-entry that fails explicitly, deterministically and bounded. The real chain additionally proves that the reroute request is consumed by the real 219 route port and produces `['lane-in','lane-b','lane-out']`, that the episode releases to the ordinary 045 `FOLLOW` when the vehicle proceeds, and that the MANUAL window keeps no AUTO decision while the re-entry plan reports the real 033 geometry without touching the authoritative pose. The targeted native probe on the real 033/044/045/041 fixture measures p95 `+0.0148 ms` per 10 Hz decision (p50 `+0.0179`), with all five repetitions and a deterministic decision/reroute/checksum trace preserved. See [the evidence](Evidence/054-blocked-recovery/README.md).

Limitations: the standstill, wait, cooldown, release and lateral-tolerance thresholds are frozen provisional constants that still need calibration on the real city map, and they are not profile parameters. The blocked set is caller input bounded to 64 lanes; the 041 traffic weights are not modified, so a congestion queue is only re-queried. A missed decision slice counts as stagnating, which is conservative for declaring BLOCKED. The physical reroute (041/219), the physical placement (030, including its explicit-seat requirement) and the drive back to the lane (046–049/024) are all caller-owned and are not exercised here against a native Rapier world. No browser, GPU, FPS, integrated-game or hardware acceptance is claimed, and the calibration labels remain provisional.

## What it is not

It is not a new state machine and it does not extend the 045 reason set: 045 keeps owning the state and the closed reason set, and this module only raises `routeBlocked`. It is not a route search, a fleet dispatcher or a traffic predictor: the reroute goes through the real 041 planner and the real 219 queue, and the blocked set and traffic weights stay explicit caller inputs. It is not a physics controller and does not decide the manoeuvre: `DRIVE_TO_LANE` is geometry plus an explicit request, never a steering, brake or pose command. It does not teleport, does not place a body and does not bypass 030's native inspection, footprint/support/occupancy checks or its explicit-seat requirement. It does not infer an available gap from the absence of local traffic and does not convert a red signal, a stop rule or a waiting driver into a blockage.
